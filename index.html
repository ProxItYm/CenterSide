The desk

Everything a support desk should be, inside Discord.

No third-party dashboard your members will never open. The ticket opens where the community already lives — and the bot already knows the answer.

Panels in one click

Dropdown categories, button panels, per-category intake forms. Learned once with /setup, posted with /panel — members pick a lane instead of guessing where to ask.

Server memory

Feed it your description, rules, roles, pricing, hours and FAQs. Every answer cites the pack it came from — no invented policies, no hallucinated refunds.

Private by default

Tickets live in threads or dedicated channels with per-role visibility. No leaked addresses, no accidental screenshots in #general.

Transcripts that hold up

Every close writes a plain-text transcript to your log channel and DMs a copy to the opener. Attachments, embeds, internal notes included.

Staff tooling

Claim, escalate, add users, private notes. The bot handles the ticket first; a human is pinged only when it runs out of answers.

Numbers, not vibes

Open tickets, resolution time, volume by lane and a staff leaderboard. Know which lane is on fire without asking anyone.

Set up with one command

Run /setup and it builds the Tickets category, creates a private #transcripts channel locked to your staff role, and asks four plain questions about your server. No settings maze, no channel IDs to paste — the bot provisions what it needs and tells you what it did.

Handoff, not hijack

One message and the bot goes silent for that ticket. Staff take over, the transcript keeps recording, and the bot resumes only when the human closes it.

Welcome embeds, built in

No embed builder to fight. /welcome writes the embed itself — avatar, member number, join timestamp, your line of copy — and makes #welcome if you do not have one.

Setup

Three steps. About two minutes.

You don't configure CenterSide with a 40-field settings page. You write it a briefing, like you would for a new staff member.

01

Invite the bot

Grant Manage Channels, Manage Roles, Send Messages and Embed Links. Everything else stays untouched.

02

One command

Run /setup. It creates the Tickets category, makes a private #transcripts channel, and asks four plain questions about your server.

03

Post the panel

Run /panel in #support. Members pick a lane, get a private channel, and CenterSide answers. That's the desk.

Command reference

Every command, written out.

Eight commands, each with its exact options and an example — this page is the manual, so the bot does not need to be a maze of slash commands to learn.

/setupadmin

Opens the question editor — up to five questions members answer before a ticket opens — then creates the Tickets category, the transcript channel and the panel.

No options. Reopen it to change the questions.

/describeadmin

Replies with a private page served by your bot. One form: server name, what the server is, tone, hours, hard facts, escalation triggers and every saved answer.

Required once before any other command works.

/editadmin

Changes one part of the setup after the fact. Every option is optional; pass only what you want to move.

  • log channel — where transcripts and staff notes go
  • category — where ticket channels are created
  • staff role — toggles that role on or off
  • welcome channel — where welcome embeds post
  • briefing — reopens the description wizard

/edit log:#transcripts

/paneladmin

Opens the ticket-option editor — up to five lanes of your own naming — then posts the dropdown in the current channel and remembers where it went, so the bot can link straight to it.

No options. Leave the form blank to keep the four defaults.

/welcomeadmin

Posts a bot-built welcome embed — avatar, member number, join time, your line of copy. Creates #welcome if it is missing.

  • message — the line under the greeting ({user}, {server}, {count})
  • channel — post somewhere else
  • off — disable welcome messages

/welcome message:Hey {user}, read the rules and say hi

/statsadmin

Open tickets, closed tickets, average resolution time, volume by lane, and a staff leaderboard of who is actually carrying the desk.

No options.

Everything else is a button on the ticket

Claim — the bot stops answering, you own it Add user — pull a member in by id or mention Escalate — ping the staff role with the thread attached Close — plain-text transcript to the log, DM to the opener, channel deleted (resolved tickets close themselves)
